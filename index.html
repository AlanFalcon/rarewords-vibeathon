Letters draw without replacement, and word draws skip registered words. In this demo world every letter and “-” is already minted (vowels and “-” by the builder vault, consonants by other players), so a letter roll falls through to a common word. Every word is a 1/1. Forging is permissionless: any registered word can be an input, both inputs survive, and each input’s owner earns 500 of the 1,000 RF fee.
